# Smart Pothole Detection 🚧🤖

### AI-Powered Road Health Monitoring & Predictive Maintenance System

Smart Pothole Detection is an intelligent road infrastructure monitoring solution that combines an autonomous rover, computer vision, GPS tracking, and AI-driven analytics to detect potholes, assess road conditions, and prioritize maintenance activities.

The system enables authorities to identify road defects in real time, monitor infrastructure health, and make data-driven repair decisions to improve road safety and reduce maintenance costs.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

Road potholes are a major cause of accidents, traffic congestion, vehicle damage, and inefficient infrastructure maintenance. Traditional inspection methods are manual, expensive, and often fail to provide timely information.

Smart Pothole Detection addresses this challenge through an autonomous rover that continuously scans roads, identifies potholes, evaluates their severity, and provides actionable insights through a centralized dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Features

### 🚗 Autonomous Road Health Rover

* Patrols roads and captures real-time road condition data
* Equipped with camera, GPS, and sensor modules
* Operates with minimal human intervention

### 🧠 AI-Based Pothole Detection

* Detects potholes using Computer Vision models
* Identifies road surface anomalies
* Classifies potholes by severity level

### 📍 Precise Location Mapping

* Records exact GPS coordinates
* Tracks pothole locations on a digital dashboard
* Enables location-specific maintenance planning

### ⚠️ Severity & Risk Assessment

* Categorizes potholes into:

  * Low
  * Medium
  * High
  * Critical
* Estimates accident risk and road safety impact

### 🏗️ Contractor & Road Information Tracking

* Stores road construction details
* Displays contractor information
* Maintains road history and maintenance records

### 🤖 AI Repair Prioritization Engine

The system recommends repair priorities based on:

* Pothole severity
* Traffic density
* Accident probability
* Road age
* Historical defect patterns

### 📊 Interactive Dashboard

* Live road monitoring
* Pothole visualization
* Severity analytics
* Real-time alerts
* AI-generated maintenance recommendations
